# PRODUCT — HONEY, I Do LLC

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (one `index.html`, photos in `img/`), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (a one-page site for a local trade)

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in the business's area (609 area code, central/southern New Jersey; exact towns unknown) who see a Facebook group post or link on their phone and want a shower re-caulked or a small repair done.
- **Secondary:** the owner of HONEY, I Do LLC, who receives the site for free and should recognise their own business in it.
- Inferred: customers open the link inside Facebook's in-app browser, one-handed, often on cellular.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook ad reader into a phone call or text. The business currently has no website, no reviews and no Maps listing; its only channel is a text-only Facebook post with a phone number.

## Positioning
- Their own words: "Anyone in need to a shower refresh? New caulk can make all the difference call us today 609-431-0036 No job to big. No job to small" (Facebook group posts, 2026-10-21).
- The brand is the pun: a "honey-do list", answered "Honey, I do." Legal name kept exactly as published: **HONEY, I Do LLC**.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 609-431-0036 (published). Texting: **unknown** (the ads say "call us today"). The site offers text as a path but the phone number is always present.
- No email, no booking link, no hours, no prices, no owner name.
- **No location may be published** (brief: no towns given). The H1 therefore carries no place name.
- No photos of their work, no logo, no reviews, no licence or insurance claims — none may be invented.

## Brand commitments
- Voice: casual, punchy, "we/us" (the ads say "call us today").
- No logo: the name is set as a wordmark. Colour is inferred from the name (honey gold on navy).

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook page: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100067576410105
- Three identical ads posted to three Facebook groups on 2026-10-21.
- Nothing else (no photos, reviews, prices, credentials).

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen says what they do and how to reach them.
2. Only facts from the brief; every inference marked in HANDOFF.md.
3. Generated photos only illustrate the service (a clean, freshly caulked bathroom), never presented as their jobs.
4. Ready to hand over: no demo or sample wording.
